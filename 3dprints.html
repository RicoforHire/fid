Here are some 3D prints that I made in high school using Tinkercad, a user friendly 3D modeling tool.

  • On the right is an articulated sensory toy of a husky silhouette I found online.
  • On the top is a statuette of an anthorphomorphic animal with a round plate on its feet to make it stand upright (without the plate, the statuette would not be able to stand on its own).
  • On the left is a simplified blocky model of a Chevrolet Lumina, which is the car that you get to play as in the 1990s arcade video game "Daytona USA".
